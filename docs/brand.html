Brand kit

One design system. Any brand.

The site starts from the Ethical Tech CoLab design system. Every colour, typeface, radius and glow is a CSS variable. A brand states a few core tokens, and the theme engine derives the rest and enforces WCAG contrast. Add ?brand= to any link to dress a demo.

01 · Presets

Lab themes and neocloud costumes.

Each card is drawn in its own theme. The neocloud and AI-lab presets approximate public colours and type for demo dressing only. Proprietary typefaces are swapped for open-licensed look-alikes, and no logos are used.
