        Method

        Assumptions, in the open

        Processing time and coordination wait are separate. Each human task independently samples a task-specific mixture: same-day reply, email follow-up, finding a meeting slot, or rescheduling. Each regime uses a triangular distribution (min, mode, max) in elapsed calendar days. The weights below are explicit synthetic demo assumptions, not measured or calibrated probabilities.

        Previously, RFQ / award was bounded at 5–16 days, PO acknowledgement at 1.5–8 days, and human decisions at 1–7 days, with a weekly planning cycle. Those narrow independent draws and shared hall / power bottlenecks made the aggregate curves look like a static offset. The revised quote, acknowledgement, screening and hand-off processing ranges exclude the communication wait to avoid counting it twice. Calendar waits can now reach two weeks for a meeting or four weeks after rescheduling; physical bottlenecks can still hide delays that fit inside available slack.

        Task keys include the line, stage and re-source attempt; human decisions and crew / acceptance tasks have their own keys. Physical and administrative randomness have separate seeds. Change ADMIN or COORDINATION in assets/js/sim/engine.js; test/sim.test.mjs checks replay, variance and shared physical draws. Independent calendar waits omit correlated organisational congestion, weekends / holidays and explicit participant calendars; use observed coordination logs before treating these presets as a benchmark.

        Honest limits
        • Every supplier, site, contract, quantity and event is synthetic. Places are real so distances are honest; nothing describes a real company's supply chain.
        • No data is included. Screening, ownership and trade signals are simulated. Each log line shows the canonical capability mapped to the active provider's native tool name where one is published, or the canonical name marked ~ where it is not. Switching provider changes the labels, never the outcome.
        • The legacy run is a synthetic model of an email-and-approvals operating model, not a calibrated measurement of any organisation.
        • One rack is one 72-GPU rack-scale system; costs and contract rates are illustrative round numbers.
        • Not an operational tool. It is built to make an argument testable, not to plan a real build.
