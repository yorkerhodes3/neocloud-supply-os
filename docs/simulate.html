        Method

        Assumptions, in the open

        Administrative latencies are triangular distributions (min, mode, max). Physical durations are shared between the runs. Change them in assets/js/sim/engine.js; the tests in test/sim.test.mjs pin the invariants.

        Honest limits
        • Every supplier, site, contract, quantity and event is synthetic. Places are real so distances are honest; nothing describes a real company's supply chain.
        • No Sayari data is included. Screening, ownership and trade signals are simulated using Sayari's documented risk-factor categories and MCP tool names.
        • The legacy run is a model of an email-and-approvals operating model, calibrated from public practice, not a measurement of any organisation.
        • One rack is one 72-GPU rack-scale system; costs and contract rates are illustrative round numbers.
        • Not an operational tool. It is built to make an argument testable, not to plan a real build.
        Lineage
        • Built on the CoLab 3D agentic evacuation simulation: deck.gl over a basemap, agents with lifecycle states, independent axes, presets as written claims, URL-reproducible runs, camera orbit and tested invariants.
        • Supplier negotiations live in the negotiation room, modelled on the CoLab diplomatic simulator and the Diplomacy Table.
        • The operating model, the 36 desks and the 16 playbooks come from CONCEPT-IDEA.md.
